Hazard 2 audit (GRPO reward path)

I inspected the GRPO reward pipeline at the pinned NeMo RL commit. Advantage is computed
as `(r - baseline) / (std + 1e-6)`, which is sign-agnostic and safe for `[-1, 1]` rewards.
The dangerous operation is `grpo.scale_rewards()`: it clamps the source range to
`[source_min, source_max]` before remapping, and its default `source_min: 0.0` would collapse
every `-1.0` gate onto the value of doing nothing. That call defaults to `enabled: false`
and the repo's tests assert the shipped configs keep it disabled. The one remaining
operational hazard is to exercise a gated rollout and confirm it receives the most
negative advantage in its group (carry-forward item 4e).

The most promising lead to follow is `docs/guides/environments.md` in the pinned NeMo RL
clone: it documents `terminate_on_evaluation: False` and shows the Environment interface
taking state/action history rather than a single completion. If that holds at the
source, ADR 0007 may be a wiring task rather than a full adapter rewrite. Verify the
source before relying on this lead.

Things to carry forward

Resource limits are both containment and throughput controls: on a single GPU a runaway
rollout stalls the whole synchronous GRPO group, and `wall_clock_s` is the operational
metric that bounds the tail. The `timeout` gate being a scored failure rather than a hang
is what makes the limits safe to rely on in production.
